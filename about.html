Our Story

Rooted in the Rockies

Summit Grounds was born from a simple idea: great coffee deserves a great view, and every book deserves a reader who finds it by chance.

We're the Hartley family — Marcus, Claire, and their teenage twins who've been running the register since they were tall enough to reach it. We roast our beans small-batch, bake our pastries at dawn, and hand-curate every single title on our shelves.

You'll find us tucked off Main Street in Silverthorne, with a wood-burning stove, mismatched armchairs, and a front window that looks straight up at the Ten Mile Range. Come for the espresso, stay for the chapter you didn't plan to start.
